This mod combines original Megabyte Punch ASCII glyphs with BoutiqueBitmap 9x9
Bold 1.93 (Add Spacing) for Chinese glyphs.  The menu uses ACKNOWTT ASCII and
the dialogue proof uses Visitor TT2 BRK ASCII; both use BoutiqueBitmap Bold for
Chinese. BoutiqueBitmap is copyright Scott0107000 and licensed under the SIL
Open Font License, Version 1.1. See FONT_LICENSE.txt.
